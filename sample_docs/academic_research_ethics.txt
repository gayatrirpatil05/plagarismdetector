Academic research ethics demands strict adherence to rigorous intellectual honesty and authentic scholarly attribution. Plagiarism represents the appropriation of another author's thoughts, ideas, or words without appropriate citation, misleading readers regarding origin. Direct copying, paraphrasing without source attribution, and patchwriting constitute violations of academic integrity policies. Blind peer review ensures objective methodological evaluation and reduces confirmatory bias prior to scientific journal publication. Authors are expected to declare potential conflicts of interest, register experimental protocols, and maintain publicly verifiable raw datasets.
