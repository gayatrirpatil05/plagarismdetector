Quantum computing is a rapidly-emerging technology that harnesses the laws of quantum mechanics to solve complex problems. A quantum computer is a device that processes information using quantum bits or qubits rather than classical binary digits. Quantum computers exploit physical phenomena such as superposition, quantum interference, and quantum entanglement. Superposition allows qubits to exist in multiple computational states simultaneously, exponentially increasing processing capacity. Entanglement creates strong correlations between qubits even across physical distances. Applications include drug discovery, cryptographic security, material science, and complex financial optimization models.
