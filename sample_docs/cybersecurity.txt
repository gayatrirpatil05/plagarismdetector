Cybersecurity involves protecting computer systems, networks, and sensitive digital assets from malicious unauthorized intrusions. Contemporary defense strategies rely heavily on zero-trust architecture, multi-factor authentication, and robust end-to-end cryptographic encryption standards. Ransomware campaigns target critical infrastructure and academic institutions, demanding substantial cryptocurrency payments to restore encrypted access. Security operations centers utilize automated anomaly detection and behavioral telemetry to intercept zero-day vulnerabilities before exploitation occurs. Regular security audits, penetration testing, and employee phishing awareness training represent fundamental components of resilient organizational posture.
