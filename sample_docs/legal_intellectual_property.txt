Intellectual property law protects intangible creations of human intellect including inventions, literary works, and artistic designs. Copyright grants creators exclusive legal rights to reproduce, distribute, and display original works of authorship. Patents protect novel technological inventions and industrial processes from unauthorized commercial exploitation for specified time periods. Trademarks safeguard brand symbols, commercial logos, and business names to prevent consumer confusion in commercial markets. Fair use doctrines establish legal exceptions permitting limited excerpt reproduction for commentary, scholarship, and educational analysis.
