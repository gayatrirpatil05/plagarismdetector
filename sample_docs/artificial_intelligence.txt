Artificial intelligence is the ability of a computer system to perform tasks that normally require human intelligence. Machine learning is a branch of artificial intelligence in which computers learn patterns from data instead of following fixed rules. Deep learning uses neural networks with many layers to recognise images, speech and language. Today, artificial intelligence is used in healthcare, banking, education and transport. Modern generative models can compose essays, generate code, and answer complex reasoning problems across various specialized disciplines. However, challenges regarding algorithmic bias, hallucinations, and copyright transparency remain active subjects of international debate.
