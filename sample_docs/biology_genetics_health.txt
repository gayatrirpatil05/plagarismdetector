Genetics is the branch of biology concerned with the study of genes, genetic variation, and heredity in organisms. Deoxyribonucleic acid or DNA carries the hereditary instructions used in the growth, development, and reproduction of all known living organisms. Cellular respiration transforms biochemical energy from nutrients into adenosine triphosphate to power cellular activities. Photosynthesis enables plants to synthesize organic compounds from carbon dioxide and water using light energy. Immunological systems protect biological organisms against pathogenic bacteria, viruses, and cellular mutations.
