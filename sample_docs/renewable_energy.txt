Renewable energy technologies capture naturally replenishing environmental resources to generate clean electricity and thermal energy. Solar photovoltaic panels convert incident photons into direct current via the photoelectric effect with advancing multi-junction efficiency. Modern offshore wind turbines harness high-velocity oceanic winds, producing gigawatt-scale power through high-efficiency permanent magnet generators. Long-duration energy storage systems, including utility-scale lithium-iron-phosphate batteries and pumped hydroelectric storage, address grid intermittency challenges. Green hydrogen produced via water electrolysis offers zero-emission decarbonization pathways for heavy transportation and chemical manufacturing sectors.
