Climate change refers to long-term shifts in temperatures and weather patterns. Burning fossil fuels like coal, oil and gas is the main driver of climate change because it releases greenhouse gases into the atmosphere. Rising global temperatures cause glaciers to melt and sea levels to rise. Many countries are now investing in solar and wind power to reduce their carbon emissions. Extreme weather events including floods, droughts and severe heatwaves have become increasingly frequent worldwide. International agreements such as the Paris Accord emphasize the necessity of keeping global warming well below two degrees Celsius. Rapid transition toward sustainable agricultural practices and deforestation mitigation is critical for preserving planetary biodiversity.
