About SemanticEye

SemanticEye is an enterprise document integrity platform engineered to solve both unauthorized copy-pasting/patchwriting and synthetic AI generation in a unified, transparent interface.

Semantic Vector Alignment

Converts sentences into contextual vectors combined with character sequence alignment to detect paraphrasing and word substitution.

Multi-Signal Statistical Heuristics

Calculates sentence length cadence, vocabulary entropy, Type-Token Ratio, and formulaic discourse transition markers.

Authenticity Certification

Generates cryptographic document hashes (SHA-256) and formal audit reports ready for printing or PDF archiving.

Privacy & Security Policy

Enterprise Data Governance & Ephemeral Processing

In-Memory Processing

All submitted texts are processed ephemerally in RAM. Documents are never saved, published, or used for AI model training.

SHA-256 Verification

Reports generate cryptographic SHA-256 document hashes to verify certificate authenticity without storing raw text.

Sanitized File Extraction

Uploaded PDF, DOCX, and TXT files are sanitized server-side with strict byte limits to prevent security risks.

Transparent Signals

Provides probability scores with sentence-level visual audit evidence for trustworthy, fair human review.
